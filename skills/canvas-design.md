# Canvas Design System

## The Canvas Philosophy
1. **The Whole is Visible**: The primary interface fits completely inside the viewport without unnecessary scrollbars. High density without clutter.
2. **Deterministic Geometry**: Every panel, card, and dock has a dedicated coordinate region.
3. **Zero-Overlap Guarantee**: Panes and drawers snap cleanly to dock edges or modal overlays.
4. **Proportional Harmony**: Standard aspect ratios (16:9, 4:3, responsive preview containers) maintain visual balance across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.
